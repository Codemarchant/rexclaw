`BUNDLED`). All are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication): no attribution
required, credited anyway. All but one come from Freesound; each was cut to
the useful part, faded at the ends, peak-normalised and encoded as MP3
(which every browser decodes), and the licence link on each source page was checked in the page's
HTML on 2026-11-14.
