# Pet sounds: provenance

Short animal calls: cat, dog, frog, parrot, owl and rabbit, and the rideable
horse, cow, camel and elephant. All are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):
no attribution required, credited anyway. All come from Freesound; each was
cut to one call, faded at the ends,
peak-normalised and encoded as mono MP3 (which every browser decodes), and
the licence link on each source page was checked in the page's HTML on
2026-11-24.
