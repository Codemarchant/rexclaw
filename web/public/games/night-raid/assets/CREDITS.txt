Rexmaw Raids — credits
======================

Images (made for Rexclaw)
-------------------------
Every image in this folder was generated in-house for Rexclaw with OpenAI
image generation, driven through the Codex CLI, then trimmed, resized and
converted to WebP by hand. No third-party image files are used.

  cover.webp                        library tile and title backdrop (the
                                    Rexmaw firing a full broadside across a
                                    split day/night sea: a sunlit merchant
                                    under fire on the left, moonlit fog and a
                                    ghostly ship on the right)
  assets/img/logo.webp              the REXMAW RAIDS logotype
  assets/img/parchment.webp         tileable parchment for the panels, the
                                    bay chart and the ship's log (from Night Helm)
  assets/img/moon.webp              the moon sprite (from Night Helm)
  assets/img/kraken_eye.webp        the Kraken's eye (from Night Helm)

  Ship textures (Rexmaw Raids), generated the same way (OpenAI image
  generation through the Codex CLI), made seamless by an offset-and-feather
  blend, resized and packed into small WebP atlases with Pillow:
  assets/tex/hull.webp              dark weathered oak hull planking | pale
                                    painted planks (tinted per ship in the
                                    shader for the gun-port strakes)
  assets/tex/deck.webp              sun-bleached deck planks
  assets/tex/sails.webp             clean sailcloth | weathered, patched
                                    canvas with torn holes in its alpha
  assets/tex/flags.webp             four invented flags: a navy ensign (a
                                    saltire and an anchor), a merchant
                                    company's stripes, a pirate's black flag,
                                    the Gloam's eye

Music (assets/music/)
---------------------

(2026-10-08). All were trimmed, made to loop seamlessly (the stings cut to
their opening phrase or natural end), loudness-normalised (beds -16 LUFS,
stings -14 LUFS) and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis 112 kbps for the game.

Sound
-----
All of Night Raid's sound effects (the layered cannons near and far, near
misses, splinters, fire, rain and thunder, the maelstrom, the Kraken, the
ship's bell and the rest), the bay's ambience, the short musical stingers
(a ship sunk, a chest, an objective...) and the fallback music (the four
tracks in js/audio/tracks.js) are procedural: synthesised in the browser with
the Web Audio API (pre-rendered with OfflineAudioContext) by the game's own
code and the games kit's music.js. The
crew's cameo lines and the companion's reaction lines are Rexclaw's own
text-to-speech recordings.
